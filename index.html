Why is ceramic tint better?

Ceramic film uses non-metallic nano-ceramic particles instead of dye or metal. That means stronger infrared heat rejection, up to 99% UV protection, sharper clarity, no fading or purpling over time and no interference with phone, GPS or radio signals.

What’s the difference between ceramic, carbon and dyed tint?

Dyed film mainly darkens glass and can fade over time. Carbon film blocks more heat and won’t fade, but ceramic rejects the most heat at any given shade while staying clear and signal-friendly.

Does ceramic tint have to be dark to keep my car cool?

No. Ceramic film rejects heat through its particles, not just its darkness, so even a lighter shade can keep your cabin noticeably cooler. That matters in California, where front side windows have strict tint limits.

Is ceramic tint worth it in San Diego?

For Southern California drivers, yes. Year-round sun means more heat, glare and UV exposure. Ceramic tint keeps the cabin cooler, eases the load on your A/C and protects your interior from fading.

How long does ceramic tint installation take?

1TakeTints’ website says most tint jobs take 1.5–3 hours depending on the vehicle and the number of windows. Existing-tint removal can add time, so confirm timing for your vehicle with the shop.

How do I care for my new ceramic tint?

The shop recommends waiting 3–5 days before rolling your windows down so the film can cure. Clean the inside glass with a soft microfiber cloth and ammonia-free cleaner, and follow the instructions given for your installation.
